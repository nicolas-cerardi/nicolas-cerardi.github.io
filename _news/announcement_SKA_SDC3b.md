The summary paper of the SKA Data Challenge 3b: Inference has been submitted to MNRAS and is available on [ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.25823)!
Our Swedish-Swiss SEarCH collaboration participated and achieved remarkable results. In particular, I lead the "Traditional SEarCH" method which ranked **3rd** on the PS2 data. For more information on our method, see our paper published in [MNRAS](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/549/1/stag779/8662579)
